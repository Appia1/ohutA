Quick answers to common OHUT Exchange questions.

Log in to your OHUT Exchange account and navigate to the Wallet section to view your available wallet balances and transactions.
Navigate to Wallet, select Funding Account, choose Send, enter the recipient's registered email address, specify the amount and confirm using your transaction PIN.
To complete your GRAM (TON) withdrawal, you need to have sufficient GRAM in your keeper wallet to cover the required blockchain network fees. Open your Keeper Wallet and copy your TON receiving address. Return to OHUT Exchange, select the GRAM withdrawal option, enter your receiving address and follow the withdrawal instructions displayed. Blockchain transactions require network fees to be processed. You need to have sufficient GRAM (TON) in your compatible external TON wallet to cover the required network fee. Check the transaction details before proceeding. Click here to watch the video tutorial
OHUT Exchange does not sell externally sourced GRAM. Users should obtain the required tokens through their preferred supported channels.
USDT withdrawals are available only in supported American regions and may be subject to account verification and other eligibility requirements.
GRAM withdrawal services are available to eligible users in supported African and American regions. Availability is subject to platform requirements.
GRAM withdrawals typically take less than 10 minutes, although processing times may vary depending on network conditions and account verification.
Some accounts may undergo additional verification or security reviews. Follow the instructions provided in your account notifications. Review updates are typically communicated within 24 hours.
KYC means Know Your Customer. It is an identity verification process used to confirm a user's identity and help meet applicable platform requirements.
Contact official OHUT Exchange Support for guidance on the available PIN recovery process.
Keep your login credentials and transaction PIN private. Never share your passwords, OTPs or wallet recovery phrases with anyone. Always use official OHUT channels.
